About

Why Verde exists

Robinhood Chain is still new, and its community coins are just getting started. Verde is built to be one of the first names people on this chain actually remember — launched fairly, priced by the open market, and run in public.

01

Fair launch, no insiders

There was no presale, no private round and no team allocation. Nobody got tokens cheaper than the public pool price — everyone buys from the same market.

02

Fixed supply, no mint

03

Run in the open

FAQ

Questions people actually ask

When does Verde launch?

The launch date and contract address will be announced on X (@verdeonhood) and posted in the contract box on this page at the same time. Until that box shows a real address, the token is not live and anything trading under the VERDE name is a copy.

What chain is Verde on?

Robinhood Chain — an Ethereum Layer 2 with Chain ID 4663. Gas is paid in ETH. VERDE shown on any other network is not this token.

Is there a tax on buys or sells?

No. The tax is 0% on both sides. You only pay the standard DEX fee and a small gas fee in ETH.

Was there a presale or team allocation?

Neither. No presale, no private sale, and 0% team allocation. 90% of the supply goes into the liquidity pool at launch.

Can more VERDE be minted later?

No. The supply is fixed at 1,000,000,000 and the contract has no mint function.

Is this financial advice?

No. VERDE is a community meme token with no promised returns. Prices can go to zero. Only put in money you are fully prepared to lose, and always verify the contract address before you trade.
